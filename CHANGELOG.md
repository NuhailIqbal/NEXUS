## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

First tracked release. Baseline for everything built so far: the agent builder, outbound campaigns (dialing
strategies, calling hours and schedules, redial and follow-up rounds), the inbound receptionist, recordings and
transcripts, call events and callbacks, Google Calendar tools, bring-your-own carriers, call consumption and
lead ROI reports, the dashboard home, the in-app assistant, and billing.
