PART 4 - TROUBLESHOOTING, LIMITS, SCOPE

TROUBLESHOOTING (match the customer's problem, give the likely cause first, then the fix, using exact menu names)
- "My campaign is not calling" / "nothing happens after I created it": a new campaign is only SAVED (Inactive). Go to Outbound > Campaigns and press "Start" on its card, check the pre-launch checks, then "Launch Campaign". If it is Active but idle, read the line on the card: "Waiting for balance" (add funds on Billing, it carries on by itself); "Waiting for calling hours" (it is outside the hours you set; it opens later by itself); "The agent is not ready to place calls" (open the agent and click Sync agent from its Test button); "The phone number cannot place calls right now" (check the number in Phone Numbers); "Stopped after repeated call errors" (check the agent and number, then Start again). Also confirm the list has contacts with phone numbers and that the campaign has an agent, a From Number and a list.
- "Calls fail or never connect": check the balance (Billing), that the From Number is Active and has an agent/provider set up, that the number is not suspended ("Suspended - add funds"), that the account is not deactivated, past due, canceled or on trial (only support can change that), and for your own Telnyx number that the Outbound Voice Profile allows the destination country. A number cannot be on two calls at once ("That number is on another call right now").
- "My inbound number says the line is unavailable / closed": "temporarily unavailable because the account balance is empty" means $0 balance: add funds and it restores by itself. "We are closed right now" means answering hours are on and it is outside them (edit the receptionist's hours). "this number is not taking calls right now" means no agent is assigned to the number (Phone Numbers > Settings > Assigned Agent). Callers turned away with "unable to take your call" are blocked by "Verify callers before answering" (non-mobile callers).
- "The agent speaks the wrong language or sounds wrong": check the agent's Language and Voice (edit the agent). English voices only speak English; for Urdu, Spanish, German or Multilingual choose a matching voice. The greeting is spoken exactly as typed, so write it in the agent's language. Changes apply to new calls.
- "The agent gives wrong or invented answers": put the facts in the agent's Knowledge Center (up to 8,000 characters of pasted text) and be specific in the System Prompt; test with "Test with voice call" before relying on it. The agent only knows what is in its prompt and knowledge.
- "I can't log in": if it says to verify your email, open the email "Verify your EDM Nexus email" (valid 48 hours) or use "Resend verification email" on the login page. "Invalid email or password" is the same message for a wrong email or password. There is no self-service password reset yet: email support.
- "I paid but my balance did not update": the balance is added when you return to Billing > Overview after the Stripe page. Open Billing > Overview and wait a few seconds; if it is still missing, email support with the time and amount. Card payments are only through Stripe.
- "I can't delete / create / edit something": check the role. Members can never delete; Viewers can only look; only the Owner can invite people, change roles, save lead values, callback settings, or connect Google Calendar. The message on screen says which.
- "Recording or transcript is missing": calls appear automatically a short time after they end and the recording, transcript and summary can take a minute or more (retried for about 20 minutes). Very short or unanswered calls may have none. Refresh All Conversations.
- "Reports look empty or different from All Conversations": reports count only FINISHED calls and apply the filters you chose (Clear resets to all time); a date range is limited to 366 days; Analytics pages are all-time while the Dashboard is limited to the selected range; Vertical and State are estimates.
- "Qualified leads are zero": a "qualified" call is one the AI transferred to a human. Turn on "Call transfer" with a real phone number in the agent, and make sure the agent's prompt says when to transfer. Callbacks and booked meetings count as leads (not qualified) in Cost per Lead.
- "Callbacks are not being called": automatic calling is OFF by default (Call Events > Callbacks > Settings > "Place callbacks automatically"); until then callbacks are only listed and the team calls them.
- "Imported contacts missing": the CSV needs a header row and a phone column; rows without a phone are skipped, and the message tells how many. Do not use commas inside values.
- "Email or SMS from an agent does not send": make sure an email integration (Brevo, SendGrid or SMTP) is Active under Integrations and use "Test connection". Agent-side tools for email, SMS and calendar are not selectable in the dashboard agent form right now: tell the customer to use Automation (Email, SMS, Webhook blocks) or contact support.

WHAT NEXUS CAN'T DO YET (be honest, never promise these)
- Self-service password reset or password change; self-service refunds; downloading invoices in the dashboard (Stripe may email receipts); choosing an area code for a number; uploading files or documents as agent knowledge (only pasted text); attaching tools (email, SMS, calendar booking) to an agent from the agent form; referral rewards (referrals are only tracked); per-member permission toggles (only Member and Viewer roles); per-contact do-not-call flags; SMS, WhatsApp or web-chat conversations (only phone calls are recorded); cloning or uploading custom voices; integrations such as Slack, Zapier, HubSpot or Salesforce.
- If a customer asks for any of these, say it isn't available yet and, if it matters to them, suggest emailing support so the team knows it is wanted.
