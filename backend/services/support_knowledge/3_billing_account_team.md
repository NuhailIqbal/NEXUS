PART 3 - BILLING, ACCOUNT, LOGIN, TEAM, REFERRALS

BILLING AND MONEY
- NEXUS is prepaid pay-as-you-go: a wallet ("Credit balance", Billing > Overview). No plans, no subscription, no monthly platform fee.
- Each call is charged once, when it ends: (the voice service's cost of the call + an estimated phone-carrier cost) x 3. This works out to about $0.35 per minute, and Billing shows it as "Est. Rate Per Minute". It is only an estimate, because every call is billed by its actual cost. Billing is by actual seconds (not rounded up to the minute) and the charge is rounded to whole cents. Numbers that were brought in from the customer's own carrier skip the carrier part. Team members see the owner's rate. Inbound calls are billed the same way when they finish.
- Where to see charges: Billing > Call Costs (Date, Contact, Direction, Duration, Cost, Status, with a total). Billing > Transactions ("Purchase History") lists money movements only: Top-up, Phone Number, Adjustment, Welcome credit, Promo code, Refund. Individual call charges are NOT in Transactions, they are in Call Costs. "Total Charges" on the Overview is a running lifetime total, even though its caption says "This billing period".
- Credit is held in lots and spent in this order: promo credit first (it expires), then purchased credit (top-ups and auto-recharge never expire). The balance never goes below $0.
- Welcome credit: $20 (the live amount can be changed by NEXUS admins), added when the email is verified (not at sign-up), once per user, and it expires after 60 days by default. No card is needed to sign up. Team members who accept an invite do not get their own welcome credit, they use the owner's wallet.
- Adding funds: Billing > Overview > "Add to credit balance". Pick $20, $50, $100 or $250, or type a custom amount. The minimum is $20 and the maximum is $1,000 per top-up. Click "Pay", pay by card on the secure Stripe page, and you are sent back to Billing, which says "Added $X to your balance". Card details never touch NEXUS servers. The balance is credited when you return to the Billing page, so if someone paid but closed the tab before coming back, tell them to open Billing > Overview, and if the balance is still missing, email support.
- Saved cards: Billing > Payment methods. "Add payment method" opens a secure card form ("Save card"). Each card shows brand, last 4 digits and expiry, with "Set as default" and "Remove". The default card is used for auto-recharge. You cannot remove your only card while auto recharge is on: turn it off or add another card first.
- Auto recharge: Billing > Overview > "Auto recharge settings". Turn on "Enable auto recharge", set "When balance falls below" ($0 to $500, default $10) and "Bring balance up by" ($20 to $1,000, default $50). It needs a saved default card. It triggers when a charge brings the balance to the threshold or lower, and if the card is declined you get a notification ("Auto recharge failed") and it tries again on the next charge while the balance is still low. It does not retry on a timer.
- Promo codes: Billing > Promotions, type the code and click "Apply". Credit is added as promo credit. Each code works once per account. Errors you may see: "That promotion code isn't valid", "has expired", "has been fully claimed", "You've already used that promotion code".
- Phone number fees: a number bought on NEXUS costs $3 per month. It is taken from the balance when you buy, and if the balance is under $3 you pay $3 by card on the Stripe page. It renews monthly from the balance. If the balance is short at renewal, the wallet is drained to $0 and the shortfall is not charged later. A number you bring from your own carrier costs $1 per month (a hosting fee) and you pay your carrier separately. Numbers are never taken away for non-payment.
- Low balance: a yellow banner shows at $10 or less ("Low balance: $X left") and a red one at $0 ("Your credit is used up"), with an "Add Funds" button. The notification bell warns once at each of $10, $5, $1 and $0. These alerts are in-app only: there are no emails or texts. Team members see the owner's balance.
- When the balance reaches $0: starting a new call, campaign, automation call or callback is refused with "Your balance is empty. Add funds to keep making calls." A running campaign stays Active and shows "Waiting for balance"; it carries on by itself once funds are added (it needs roughly $0.35 of balance per open call). Inbound phone numbers are suspended ("Suspended - add funds"): callers hear that the line is temporarily unavailable. Everything restores automatically as soon as the balance is above $0 (top-up, promo or auto-recharge).
- Refunds, invoices: there is no self-serve refund and no invoice download in the dashboard. For a refund or a billing correction the customer must email support with what happened. Do not promise a refund.
- Account status: normal accounts are Active. Only NEXUS support can change an account to a state that blocks calling ("Your account has been deactivated", "past due", "canceled", or trial mode which allows building agents but not calling). If a customer sees one of these, they must contact support.

ACCOUNT, LOGIN AND TEAM
- Sign up (Create Account): Full Name, Email, Password (and a reCAPTCHA if shown). One account per email. You must verify your email before you can log in: click the link in the email "Verify your EDM Nexus email" (valid 48 hours, single use). If the email did not arrive, use "Resend verification email" on the login page. After verifying you are taken to the dashboard and the welcome credit is added.
- Login: Email and Password, "Sign In". Sessions last 7 days. There is NO self-serve password reset or change-password yet: "Forgot password?" tells the customer to contact their administrator. They should email support to have it reset. Sign out: the avatar menu (top right) > "Sign Out".
- Profile & Teams (sidebar): "Edit" lets you change Full name, Company and Phone. Email cannot be changed. There is no avatar upload, timezone setting or password change there.
- Roles: Account Owner (everything), Member ("can create and edit" but cannot delete anything, invite or remove people, or change roles), Viewer (read-only: can look at everything but cannot create, edit, delete, add funds or save cards). Team members share the owner's wallet, data and notifications.
- Invite someone: Profile & Teams > "Add Collaborator" > enter Email, choose "Member (can create & edit)" or "Viewer (read-only)" > "Send Invite". The invitee gets an email (valid 7 days) and clicks "Accept invite & continue" to set a password. The owner can change a role from the dropdown or remove a person with the trash icon (they are signed out within seconds). If an invite expired, remove the person and invite again. Only the owner can invite.
- Referrals (sidebar): shows your personal link (.../register?ref=CODE) with a "Copy" button, and a table of people who signed up with it, "Pending" until they verify their email, then "Verified". Referrals are only tracked. There is currently NO reward, credit or discount for the referrer or the new user, so never promise one.
- Notifications: the bell (top right) shows low-balance, auto-recharge, campaign and DNC messages plus your recent calls. Opening it marks them read. It is shared by the whole team.
