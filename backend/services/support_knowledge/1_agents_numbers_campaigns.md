PERMISSIONS: the account Owner can do everything. A Member can create and edit but can never delete anything (agents, numbers, receptionists, campaigns): they see "Sub-users cannot delete resources. Contact your account owner." A Viewer is read-only: any create, edit or start shows "Viewers have read-only access. Contact your account owner to make changes." AI AGENTS (menu: AI Agents; button "Add New Agent")
- The list shows each agent with industry, status, voice, language and created date, and buttons Test, Settings (gear = edit), Duplicate and Delete. Filters: search, status, category, date range.
- Creating an agent is a 5-step wizard. Nothing is saved until the last step's "Create Agent" button. New agents are Active.
  1. "Complete Setup": Agent Name (required); optional Website with an "Analyze" button that reads the site and fills in Main Goal and Industry (up to 10 per minute; private or unreachable sites fail with a clear message); Main Goal (required, what the agent should accomplish); a "Call transfer" switch with a Transfer Number in international format like +15551234567 (qualified calls are handed to that human phone number); Industry (required, searchable: Retail & E-commerce, Healthcare & Medical, Finance & Banking, Real Estate, Education, Travel & Hospitality, SaaS & Technology, Automotive, Insurance, Debt & Credit Services, Home Services & Contracting, Legal Services, Telecommunications & Internet, Marketing & Professional Services); Language (English, Urdu, Spanish, German, or Multilingual auto-detect; default English); Voice (19 voices, default Elliot).
  2. "Knowledge Center": one text box "Business Knowledge", up to 8,000 characters (paste FAQs, product info, policies, scripts). It is required when creating. It is added to the agent's instructions and used on every call. There is NO file or document upload: only pasted text.
  3. "Prompt Studio": the System Prompt (the agent's persona and instructions, required) and the Greeting Message (the first thing the agent says, spoken exactly as written, required). For Spanish or German write the greeting in that language.
  4. "Call Events" (optional): pick up to 20 events from your event library; the agent reports them during the call and the last one sets the call outcome.
  5. "Testing" (optional): "Test with voice call" talks to the unsaved agent live in the browser (needs microphone permission, no phone number needed), or type a "Test Message" and "Run test" for a text reply. The test uses prompt, greeting, voice and language only (not the knowledge, transfer or events).
- Editing: the gear icon opens the same 5 steps with "Save Changes"; changes go live on the agent immediately. Edit also has a Status (Active, Paused, Inactive). Turning "Call transfer" off in edit does not clear the old transfer number (known issue: tell the customer to contact support if they need it removed). The Knowledge step on edit appends new text to what is already there.
- Duplicate makes "<name> (Copy)" with the same prompt, greeting, voice, language, industry and call events (not the transfer number). Delete is permanent (Owner only); phone numbers and campaigns that used the agent stay but lose their agent.
- The list's Test button opens a live browser call with a saved agent. If it says the agent isn't set up for voice calls, click "Sync agent".
- Accounts that are deactivated, past due or canceled cannot create agents (the customer sees the message and must contact support). Trial accounts can build agents but not place calls.
- There is no "tools" picker (send email, book calendar...) in the agent form yet, and no custom tools page. Do not tell customers they can attach tools to agents from the dashboard; Call Events are how agents trigger actions.

AI VOICES (menu: AI Voices)
- A browse-and-preview catalog of 19 voices: English 13 (Elliot, Savannah, Rohan, Emma, Clara, Nico, Kai, Sagar, Godfrey, Neil, Layla, Sid, Naina), Urdu 2 (Zara, Ali), Spanish 2 (Lucia, Mateo), German 2 (Greta, Lukas). Each card has a Preview button (type sample text, "Play Preview", about 20 seconds; the browser asks for microphone access even though you don't speak) and a Favorite star kept only in your browser. You cannot create, clone or upload voices.
- The voice is chosen per agent in the agent form. English voices speak English only: if an agent's language is Spanish or German, the agent automatically uses Lucia or Greta instead, and changing the language switches the voice for you. For Multilingual use Zara, Ali, Lucia, Mateo, Greta or Lukas (the English-only voices can't read other languages). If the voice is blank it falls back to the language's default.

PHONE NUMBERS (menu: Phone Numbers; button "Buy Number")
- The table shows Number, Used In (Inbound, Outbound, Caller check), Provider (Standard, Twilio, Vonage, Telnyx), Assigned Agent, Status (Active, Inactive, "Suspended - add funds"), Purchased and Expires (the next monthly billing date), with Settings, Test and Release icons.
- Buy a Standard (NEXUS) number: Buy Number > choose Purpose (Inbound, Outbound or Both) > Service Provider "Standard" > optionally "Assign AI Agent" > Active switch (off by default, switch it on to use the number now) > Create. It costs $3 per month: taken from your balance if it covers $3, otherwise you pay $3 once on the secure Stripe page and the number is created after payment. The number is a US local number; you cannot choose an area code. Renewal is automatic every month from the balance.
- Use your own carrier (Twilio, Vonage or Telnyx): Buy Number > Service Provider = your carrier > "Connect a <carrier> account" and enter your credentials (Twilio: Account SID and Auth Token; Vonage: API Key and API Secret; Telnyx: API Key). NEXUS checks them and stores them encrypted. Then either "I already own a number" (type it as +1XXXXXXXXXX) or "Buy a new number on my <carrier> account". NEXUS charges $1 per month hosting; the carrier bills you directly for the number and call minutes. For Telnyx outbound calls, the Outbound Voice Profile must allow the countries you call.
- Number Settings (gear): change the Assigned Agent (changes where inbound calls go), see the Provider, switch "Verify callers before answering".
- "Verify callers before answering": only callers on a mobile phone reach your agent; others hear "Sorry, we are unable to take your call. Goodbye." A "Recent checks" log shows who was answered or turned away and why (mobile, landline, VoIP, not a valid number, no caller number, can't be checked). If the check itself fails or the caller has no US number, the call is let through. If the switch is locked it says "Caller verification isn't available yet. Please contact support."
- Test (play icon): a dial pad that places a real call from that number to any phone so you hear your agent. It needs an assigned agent and a balance, and it is billed like any call.
- Release (trash icon): releases the number at once with no confirmation (Owner only). Standard numbers are returned and billing stops, with no refund of the $3. For your own carrier's number, NEXUS only removes it from the platform: cancel it at your carrier yourself.
- If the balance reaches $0, inbound numbers show "Suspended - add funds" and callers hear the line is temporarily unavailable; adding funds restores them automatically.

INBOUND (menu: Inbound > AI Receptionist)
- A receptionist is an agent that answers calls to one of your numbers. "New Receptionist" is a 2-step wizard: (1) Name, Agent, and Inbound Number: "Buy a new number ($3/month)" or an existing unused number; (2) "Answering Hours": the switch "Only answer during set hours" with Time Zone (required), Days, "Answers from" and "Answers until" (end after start, no crossing midnight). Outside those hours callers hear "Sorry, we are closed right now. Please call back during our opening hours. Goodbye." Default when switched on is Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00. Without the switch it answers every call at any time.
- Each card shows the number (with a copy button), status, "Verify callers before answering", and "Open now / Closed". The gear edits name, agent, hours and status. The trash deletes the receptionist (no confirmation); the number is kept.
- Inbound > Call Logs lists every incoming call (Caller, Agent, Status, Duration, Time, refreshing every 30 seconds). Click a call for its recording, transcript, summary and sentiment.

OUTBOUND CAMPAIGNS (menu: Outbound > Campaigns; button "New Campaign")
- A campaign calls every number in a contact list with one agent from one of your numbers. It needs an agent, a "From Number" and a contact list (create lists in Database > Lists first).
- IMPORTANT: finishing the wizard ("Launch Campaign") only SAVES the campaign as Inactive and does not dial. Press "Start" on the campaign card, review the pre-launch checks, and press "Launch Campaign" there to begin calling.
- The 6 wizard steps: (1) Campaign Basics: name, agent, From Number, DNC screening. (2) Contact List. (3) Calling Hours. (4) Auto Redial. (5) Dialing Strategy. (6) Review & Launch.
- Calling Hours: "Only call during set hours" with a required Time Zone; either one schedule for all days or a custom schedule per day; up to 10 breaks per day. Outside the hours no new calls start but calls already in progress are never cut off. If the hours or a break end mid-run, the campaign shows Paused and continues by itself from the next contact when the hours reopen (the card offers "Stop auto-resume"). Without this switch the campaign can call at any time.
- Auto Redial (off by default): "Calls per number" 1-10 (default 3, the first call counts), "Wait between calls" 5-60 minutes in steps of 5 (default 10), and "Redial when" No Answer, Busy, Voicemail / Answering Machine, Call Failed / Network Error (all on by default). A number is never redialed when a person spoke with the agent, the call was transferred, the person was disqualified or asked not to be called, or the number is invalid. Redials follow the calling hours and strategy.
- Dialing Strategy: "Sequential" (default; one call at a time), "Concurrent" ("Concurrent Calls" 1-20, default 5, refilled as calls end) or "Paced" ("Calls per Minute" 1-60, default 10). "Delay between calls (seconds)" 0-60. Your account may have its own lower cap on simultaneous calls; if so the form warns you and calls run at the allowed number. A number is called once per run and never on two calls at once.
- DNC screening: needs the WhitelistData connection under Integrations (turned on account-wide). When on (default for each campaign), numbers on the do-not-call / litigation list are skipped and counted as "skipped (DNC)". If the screening service is down the campaign waits and retries rather than calling unscreened numbers.
- Pre-launch checks when you click Start: agent assigned and synced, phone number assigned and active, the list has dialable contacts, and inside calling hours. "Launch Campaign" stays disabled until all pass.
- Start / Pause / Resume: Start on a new (Inactive) campaign calls the whole list; Start on a Paused campaign resumes where it stopped so nobody is called twice. "Pause" stops new calls right away (calls in progress finish). When every contact has been reached the button reads "All contacts reached"; starting again later begins from the top of the list.
- Statuses: Active (placing calls), Paused (no new calls, place is kept; a calling-hours stop also shows Paused), Inactive (new or stopped), Completed (every contact has a completed call).
- The card shows live counters (Active now, In queue, Placed) and a state line: "Calling now", "Waiting for calling hours", "Waiting for redials", "Paused", "Idle". Other messages: "Waiting for balance: add funds and it carries on by itself" (balance too low, resumes automatically after funds are added); "The agent is not ready to place calls. Open it and sync it."; "The phone number cannot place calls right now."; "Stopped after repeated call errors. Check the agent and phone number, then start it again." The "Numbers" button shows each number's attempts, outcome and next redial.
- Common start errors and the fix: "Campaign has no contact list assigned" or "no phone number assigned" (edit the campaign and choose one); "No contacts with phone numbers in the list" (add or import contacts); "Your balance is empty. Add funds to keep making calls." (Billing); "The campaign's phone number is not active yet" (wait or re-check the number); "This campaign is already running"; "closed right now" (outside calling hours, it shows when it opens); trial, past due or canceled account messages (contact support).
- Delete (trash) removes the campaign with no confirmation (Owner only). Outbound > Call Logs lists every outbound call with recording and transcript.

- Two checklists that tick themselves from your real data. Outbound: create your first AI agent (about 5 min), get a phone number (2), add your contacts (3), launch your first campaign (10), review your first outbound calls (3). Inbound: agent, phone number, set up an AI receptionist (5), connect a number to it (2), review your first inbound calls (3). The page shows time left and an "Up next" card with a "Continue" button.
